# Testimonials: script and consent

For the owner (questionnaire 7.4: "we can arrange, need the script"). Use it
for short videos, a written quote, or both. Real people, their own words, with
written permission. Nothing on the site may be invented, paid for, or edited
to say something the person did not say.

## Who to ask

- Candidates who have **arrived and started work**, at least a month in.
  Not people still waiting for a visa: their story is not finished.
- Employers or hotel managers in the group who have hired through us.
- Never someone who has a complaint open with us, and never anyone in
  exchange for money, a discount, or a better position in selection.

## Before recording

1. Explain what it is for: the recruitment website and its social media.
2. Give them the consent form below in a language they read well, and get it
   signed before recording. Keep the signed copy.
3. Tell them they can say no, and that saying no changes nothing for their
   job or contract.

## Questions for a placed candidate (3 to 5 minutes)

Ask in their language if they prefer; we subtitle it. Let them answer in their
own words. Do not hand them lines to read.

1. What is your name, where are you from in India, and what do you do now?
2. What were you doing before you applied?
3. How did you hear about us, and what made you trust it?
4. What was the assessment like?
5. What happened between the offer and the visa? What was hard?
6. What did you pay for, and what was paid for you? (Let them answer
   honestly; do not prompt.)
7. What was the first week in Portugal like? Housing, food, paperwork.
8. What is a normal working day now?
9. What would you tell someone in India who is thinking of applying?
10. Is there anything you wish had been different?

Question 10 stays in. A testimonial that admits something was hard is more
believable, and it is the kind of feedback the team needs anyway.

## Questions for an employer or manager (2 to 3 minutes)

1. Your name, role, and the business.
2. What roles did you need to fill, and why from India?
3. How did the candidates compare with what you expected?
4. What support did they need in the first weeks?
5. Would you hire this way again?

## Rules for what we publish

- Use what they said. Cut for length, never to change the meaning.
- No claims about odds, guaranteed jobs, visas or salaries beyond the
  person's own situation. If someone says "everyone gets a job", cut it.
- Show their real first name, role and city. Surname and face only if the
  form allows it.
- Add the date recorded. Remove it from the site when they ask, within
  7 days.
- Candidates can also send their story from their dashboard ("Your move"
  page). Those wait for review in the console before anything is published.
